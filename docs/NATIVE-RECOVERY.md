# Recovered native iMessage/lock-screen system

118 historical output images and 19 renderer/configuration files were recovered and source/destination-hash checked. The public walkthrough includes 54 formatted outputs; unformatted/reference files outside the approved selection are not prerequisites for the local Canvas demo.

The [source snapshots](../archive/source/accepted-ios-recovered/) include variants.py, variant_h.py, slides.py, the iOS HTML/CSS templates and renderer helpers. They are inert references with owner-host paths removed, not a promised portable native renderer. Use the [source manifest](../catalogue/source-snapshots.json) for public snapshot hashes and transformations.

Narrative variants use a photo hook, authored fictional notification/message thread and product payoff. Variant h is an authority-list treatment. Pillow handles photo overlays; headless Chrome renders the iOS HTML at 3×. Archived typography relies on particular TikTok Sans cuts and Apple system fonts. Their exact dependencies and original app captures need separate review before a native rerun.

Source/output recovery is verified. Native rerendering is not. Simulated messages are not real private chats, and historical product/translation claims are not verified customer outcomes. Full-resolution public review copies are linked from the viewer and [asset manifest](../assets/full-resolution.json).
